📋 Condominium House Rules

Uptown Parksuites Condominium — House Rules & Regulations

👥 1BR max 4 🚭 Smoke/Vape free 🐾 No pets ⏱️ Lobby 15 min 🪪 Visitor ID to Host by 9 PM 🌙 Visitors out by 12 AM 🧳 No luggage storage
🏢 Purpose of the Building
Residential units must not be used as boarding houses, dormitories, or bed-space establishments. No business or commercial activities open to the public are allowed.
🏥 Facility Usage
The property is not a medical facility or hospital. All medical treatments must be arranged off-site with appropriate professionals. Personal medical equipment is not permitted on the property.
🚗 Car Pass Guidelines
Illegal parking is prohibited and incurs a penalty. Use the assigned parking slot during the valid period. The Green Car Pass must be returned upon exiting the parking lot at the end of its validity — lost passes incur a penalty.
🪪 Guest Requirements
Complete the Guest Information Sheet (GIS) and attach valid IDs of all guests/companions/visitors, along with the guest guideline acknowledgement, prior to arrival.
👥 Maximum Occupancy
Maximum guests & visitors per unit:
• 1BR — 4
• 2BR — 5
• 3BR — 7
This unit is a 1BR (max 4).
🎟️ Visitor Pass & Visitor Hours
Condo rules do not allow visitors after 12:00 AM (midnight). Visitor endorsement (a photo of each visitor's valid ID) must be sent to the Host no later than 9:00 PM so the front desk can be notified — same-day or late IDs may delay or prevent entry.

Unregistered guests/visitors are strictly prohibited and will be denied entry. All visitors must register with the front desk. The Visitor's Pass must be returned to the front desk; lost passes must be reported immediately to Property Management.
🧺 Unit Upkeep
Hanging clotheslines or laundry items on balconies and windows is strictly prohibited.
🔊 Noise & Fumes
Residents and guests must avoid noisy or boisterous acts that disturb the peace and order within the condominium premises.
🧳 Luggage Storage
There is no luggage storage available in the lobby or on the premises. Please make alternative arrangements for luggage before and after your stay, and always keep the mailbox securely closed.
⏱️ Lobby Usage
Please limit time in the lobby to a maximum of 15 minutes. Guests may not loiter on the premises unless accompanied by a resident or authorized representative.
🐾 Pet Policy
Guests are not allowed to bring pets onto the property.
🚭 Smoke / Vape FREE
Uptown Parksuites is a SMOKE/VAPE FREE building in compliance with E.O. 26-2017 and Taguig City Ordinance No. 15 of 2017.
🏊 Pool Guideline
Pool usage is scheduled Tuesday–Friday (excluding holidays). Weekends & holidays are exclusively for residents and unit owners. Residents may register up to 3 guests free; additional guests cost ₱300/person. Pool is closed Mondays for maintenance (moved to the next day if Monday is a holiday). Register at the front desk before use.
💆 Spa
Rate per hour for guests: ₱300.00.
🚫 Amenity Exclusiveness
Exclusive to owners/residents/tenants: Function Hall, Gym, Business Center, Game Room, Meeting Room, Daycare.
🗑️ Garbage Collection
Collection times — 1st: 9:00 AM–12:00 PM · 2nd: 5:00 PM–9:00 PM. During collection hours you may leave 1 trash bag outside by the door. Any excess trash must be thrown at the basement (MRF room). Leaving garbage in hallways, stairways, or common areas outside these rules is strictly prohibited and incurs a penalty.

👋 Checkout — before 11:00 AM

  1. Turn off all lights and the air conditioner.
  2. Seal your trash in a bag and leave it inside the unit — housekeeping handles disposal.
  3. Lock the main door on your way out.
  4. Return your Visitor's Pass to the lobby (if issued).
  5. Double-check you haven't left anything behind.
🧼 Kindly check towels, sheets, sofa & cushioned chairs for stains and let your host know, so any extra cleaning can be sorted out.
🕐 Planning an early checkout? Just message your host with the expected time.

❓ Quick FAQ

What's the Wi-Fi password?
Network GFiber_AACC6, password 9SxmwTtC. See the "Access & Wi-Fi" tab for one-tap copy.
How does check-in work?
Register at the lobby with a valid ID and the endorsement screenshot, then go to Tower 2, Unit 6D. It's self check-in using the SMART door code your host sends.
Can I have visitors?
Yes, but condo rules do not allow visitors after 12:00 AM. Send each visitor's valid ID to your Host no later than 9:00 PM for endorsement to the front desk. Unregistered visitors will be denied entry.
Where do I park?
Paid parking lot near Landers, next to Tower 1.
How does trash work?
During collection hours (9 AM–12 PM & 5 PM–9 PM) you may leave 1 trash bag outside by the door. Any excess trash must be thrown at the basement (MRF room).
Is smoking allowed?
No. Smoking/vaping is banned in the unit, balcony, and common areas, with steep penalties (₱10k / ₱20k / ₱50k).
Can I check out early?
Yes, you can — but please note some days may not be refundable, since those nights are already lost if it's on short notice.
Is the gym allowed?
Unfortunately no, the building gym is for residents/owners only. But there's an Anytime Fitness down the street at Uptown Mall (4th Flr, Tower 3) — a day pass is around ₱500. Open in Google Maps →
